# WS2: Market Sizing, Knox, Blount, Loudon, Anderson, Sevier

**Summary**
1. There are about **14,400 home sales a year** across the five counties (base case; range 12,650–16,700). Blount is sourced (2,310). Knox is inferred from Redfin monthly counts. Loudon, Anderson, and Sevier are extrapolated from single-month counts.
2. Transaction-driven mitigations: **about 1,600 a year** in the base case (range 660–3,230). That is sales × share tested × share at or above 4.0 × share that actually get mitigated.
3. Adding non-transaction demand (homeowners testing on their own, new-construction stack activation) gives **about 2,000 jobs a year in the base case** (range 760–4,500).
4. The gate's 8 jobs a month (96 a year) is **4.7% of the base market**, 12.6% of low, 2.1% of high. The 2026 sales-growth forecast (+6.8%) alone adds about 140 jobs a year, so year-1 volume doesn't strictly have to come out of incumbents' share.
5. The biggest uncertainty is the **share of transactions that include a radon test**. No local source was found. Owner action: ask 3–5 inspectors for their radon attach rate.

> Sources were read from **web-search excerpts** because direct page loads were blocked in this environment. Every figure is labeled **S** (sourced) or **E** (estimated).

With about 10 active competitors (WS3), a 5% share in 12 months is plausible for a well-run entrant but not guaranteed. In the low case, 8 jobs a month would require about one in eight of all local jobs, which is aggressive. **Base case: passes, with modest headroom. Low case: fails.**

## 4. Non-transaction demand notes

- **Knox new construction (S):** Knox adopted IRC Appendix F with a local amendment. The passive radon vent may be capped in the attic until a test shows ≥4 pCi/L, and the requirement for attic power was removed ([Knox County IRC amendments](https://www.knoxcounty.org/codes/pdfs/IRC_ammendments.pdf)). Knox homes built since the ~2007 code adoption therefore often have a ready-made stack but no fan and no outlet. That makes "activation" (add a fan and electrical) a lower-material job. The market size is **not estimated** because no permit-count source was found. See WS4 for margin; electrical cost dominates it.
- **Retests and fan replacement (recurring):** EPA recommends retesting every 2 years (from the brief). Fans typically carry 5–7 year warranties and last 10–15 years ([Rn86 FAQ](https://rn86solutions.com/radon-testing-and-mitigation-faqs/)). This revenue compounds on the owner's own installed base, so it is excluded from year-1 sizing.

## 5. Seasonality

- **S (qualitative):** most real-estate transactions happen in spring and summer, while radon readings run higher in winter under closed-house conditions ([Indoor Science](https://indoorscience.com/blog/radon-and-winter/); [Protect Environmental](https://www.protectenvironmental.com/when-are-radon-levels-the-highest/)).
- **S:** December 2025 East TN sales rose 16% month-over-month ([ETN Dec 2025 Market Pulse](https://www.etnrealtors.com/2025/12/30/2025dec-marketpulse/)), so winter isn't dead.
- **E (planning curve):** May–August ≈ 1.2× the average month and December–February ≈ 0.75×. At 8 jobs/month average, expect about 10 in peak months and about 6 in winter. January (National Radon Action Month) and the winter high-reading season are the time for direct-to-homeowner marketing, which fills the transaction trough.
