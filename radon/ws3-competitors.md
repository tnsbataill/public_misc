# WS3: Competitive Teardown

**Summary**
1. There are at least **10 active mitigators** serving Knox and Blount: 5 from the brief plus Radon't, SWAT Environmental, National Radon Defense, BGS Radon Mitigation, and Nviro-Serv. Two of the brief's names look like **one operator**: Volunteer Radon and Nviro-Serv share an address and phone number.
2. **Almost nobody publishes prices.** Radon 1 publishes ranges. The only other price points seen are third-party directory figures. Published pricing plus a fast written quote is open ground.
3. **The engineering angle is not unique.** Rn86 already markets NRPP certification, the ANSI/AARST SGM-SF-2023 standard, and quotes based on pressure field extension (PFE) testing. The owner has to differentiate on a *visible* deliverable (a written design sheet with fan-curve sizing) plus speed, not on the claim alone.
4. **Guarantees are the table stakes to match:** Radon't offers a lifetime workmanship warranty and a <2.7 pCi/L guarantee, and Radon 1 offers a price match and a "low-level guarantee."
5. **Not checked, because the sites were blocked in this environment:** Google review themes, Google Business Profile completeness, the Meta Ad Library, live search ads, and NRPP directory status for most firms. These need a 30-minute manual pass (steps in §4).

## 3. Positioning gap
Local competition splits into three groups:
- **Review-rich owner-operators** (Radon Consultants of East TN), who win on reputation.
- **One diagnostic-positioned firm** (Rn86), which owns the "engineered, standards-based" story in words.
- **Out-of-area and national lead-gen operators** (Radon 1, Radon't, SWAT, National Radon Defense), who win on guarantees and SEO volume.

None of them visibly offers a **same-day, written, priced quote from the buyer's test report and photos**, or a **published price sheet**, or a **customer-facing design document** showing suction-point layout, PFE readings, the fan-curve operating point, and before/after results. That combination fits inside a real-estate contingency window and is shareable with both agents.

Recommended position:
- Transparent published pricing bands.
- A quote within 2 business hours from report plus photos, and a written engineering design with every job.
- Guarantees that match the market floor: post-mitigation result below 4.0 and a lifetime workmanship warranty (tied to fan warranty terms).
- **Don't sell the initial transaction test.** That keeps inspectors comfortable referring (see WS5).

The strongest incumbent response would be Rn86 adding written design reports. So speed and the agent-facing workflow (WS6) have to carry the edge, not engineering claims alone.
